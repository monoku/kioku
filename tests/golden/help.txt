$ kioku help page
kioku page <verb>

Search, list and show the pages of this folder's brains, and write them.

  search     searches the brains of this folder, by meaning and by text
  list       the pages, 20 at a time, with their fields
  show       one page, with its body, its links and its history
  save       writes a page, or answers the similar pages it found instead
  update     rewrites a page's body, or one section, keeping its history
  supersede  writes a new decision and marks the old one as replaced
  forget     deletes a page; git keeps it
  confirm    records that a page is still true
  retire     marks a page as not current, kept as history
  rename     gives a page a new title; links follow
  move       moves a page to another namespace of its brain; its id stays and links follow
  link       links two related pages, each under the other's See also
  promote    writes a page again in a more shared brain and retires the original
  evolve     lists the instincts ready to become Skills, or converts one

$ kioku help brain
kioku brain <verb>

Make, clone, sync, publish and check the brains of this machine.

  create     makes a brain on this machine with the name you give it, with no remote and no network;
             personal unless --tier says
  clone      brings a brain that exists to this machine from any address, under the name it holds
  sync       pulls and pushes a brain, or every brain, wherever you are
  publish    pushes a brain that lives only on this machine to an empty repository at the address
             you give; its name stays
  list       the brains this machine knows, and the chain of this folder
  path       prints a brain's folder
  remove     takes a brain off this machine, never off the git host: deletes its clone and forgets
             it; refuses what would lose work unless --force
  log        the writes of the chain's brains since today, or since when you say
  stats      what the writes of the chain's brains add up to over a span, 30d unless you say: by
             verb, and how many saved pages were forgotten
  sources    what the brains were filled from, with the version each page was written from
  validate   checks a brain's files against the format
  lint       what to review: similar pages, quiet instincts, superseded decisions, and the rest
  language   declares the language a brain's pages are written in; the summary tells the agent
  upgrade    raises a brain to the newest format, in one commit
  refresh    rewrites the files the tool owns in a brain, or in every brain
  conflicts  the changes kept aside after a conflict with the team's, and how to resolve them

kioku help brain <verb> shows one.

$ kioku help namespace
kioku namespace <verb>

List, add, describe and rename the namespaces of a brain.

  list      the namespaces of the brains of this folder, with the pages in each
  add       declares a namespace, or one under another, and writes its index with what it is for
  describe  says what a namespace is for, in the paragraph under its index's title
  rename    gives a namespace another path, with everything under it; its folder, its pages and the
            links into it follow
